Minimotor Samples

Small games built with the Minimotor engine

Fundamentals

Focused demos — each one puts a single Minimotor module or primitive front and center.

API Lab ✨

everythingplatformernet

The flagship: the platformer that designed the current API. Tiles, ECS coins, camera follow/shake, wall jumps & dashes, scenes with a pause menu, synth audio, and live multiplayer ghosts — one file exercising the whole engine.

Minimal

Stage.initKeysLoop

Absolute minimum: a colored square that moves with arrow keys. Demonstrates Stage.init, polled Keys, and the game loop.

Scenes

ScenesTransitionsAudioUI

Menu → play → game over, with a pause overlay pushed on top. The Scenes stack (define/go/push/pop, enter/exit), fade/wipe transitions, and the UI helpers — clickable buttons, floating score pops, a draining time bar.

Sprites

Sprites.atlasAnimECS

A procedural sprite sheet baked once with Sprites.atlas, animated with Anim.sheet, and rendered through the ECS Sprite source-rect + Draw.sprites(ecs.dense(Sprites.Sprite)). Click to add more.

Bounce

LoopStorageAudio

Ball bounces off walls. Each wall hit earns a point. Best score saved with Storage.

Snake

StorageAudioGoodiesUI

Classic grid-based snake with growing tail. Collect food, avoid self-collision. Arrow or WASD.

Particles

ECSSpritesPointer

200-spark firework effect. Click to spawn. Gravity pulls sparks down. Spark texture pre-rendered with Sprites.getSprite.

Synth

AudioPointerUI

A playable synth: keyboard piano (click it too), four waveforms, octave shift — plus scheduled backing grooves with bass and drums, and a bar visualizer.

Tiles

TilesCameraInput.gamepadAudio

A scrolling tile platformer: a plain number[][] level blitted from a baked atlas, culled to the camera, with solidInRect driving the collision. Plays on keyboard or gamepad.

Juice

ParticlesCamera.shakeInput.vibrate

Impact feedback: a bouncing ball fires particle bursts, screen-shake and haptics on every hit. Click for a big one. Uses Mathf randoms for variety.

Physics

Physics2DECSCameraAudio

Real rigid bodies via the opt-in planck (Box2D) adapter: crates stack and sleep, balls bounce, a motorized paddle flings everything. Hold to pour more in.

Server Browser

UI.tableUIPointer.wheelClock

A full GUI screen from the immediate-mode UI kit: tabs, toggles, a sortable scrollable UI.table, disabled buttons and a mock refresh/join flow. No DOM.

Swept Collision

CollisionsweptAABBAudio

A projectile flies into a thin wall. Toggle between sweptAABB and a per-frame overlap test and watch fast movers tunnel straight through the naive one.

P2P Data Channel

NetWebRTC

A real WebRTC data channel, both peers in one page (no server). Move on the left; the right dot is drawn only from bytes that crossed the channel.

WebSocket Console

NetWebSocket

Connect to any WebSocket server and echo messages both ways. Net.connect with send / onMessage / state, running outside the game loop.

Clockwork Garden

ClockTweenSignalsParticles

Harvest a living rhythm garden. Timed spawns, eased buds and score/combo events are separate systems connected by Signals; survive misses and replay for a better run.

Parallax Courier

CameraMathfPointerParticles

Fly through a vast neon valley, dodge chasing drones and dock at three beacons with a smooth dead-zone camera, zoom and procedural parallax.

Asset Quest

AssetsAnimTextParticles

Load a JSON map and image manifest, collect every moon key, then reach the unlocked gate with a clean animated astronaut.

Showcase

Complete little games that compose many modules together into a full playable loop.

Breakout

ScenesECSCollisionAudio

Paddle + ball + destructible blocks. Blocks are ECS entities (queried for collision + render); Scenes drive play and a pushed game-over overlay.

Multiplayer Blobs

Net.connectInterpolatorheartbeat

A real WebSocket game: every open tab is a player, relayed by the dev server. Sends at only 20 Hz — remote players glide anyway thanks to snapshot interpolation.

Road Rivals

NetPhysics2DECSInterpolator

Two live clients in one page. Fight on foot, enter or exit your car, drift with a dynamic bicycle model, and see remote players smoothed through snapshot interpolation.

Pocket Asteroids

FullscreenGameInput.gamepadParticles

A complete Asteroids loop: rotate, thrust, wrap, fire, split rocks, clear waves and hyperspace. Mobile keeps a 16:9 frame; desktop fills its viewport.

Pixel Adventure

TilesFsmAnimAudio.Mixer

A complete platformer using real Pixel Frog CC0 art from itch.io: an autotiled terrain atlas, an Fsm-driven player, a zoomed camera decoupled from a screen-space UI, stomp combat and synthesized SFX mixed through the audio bus.

Ascent

FsmTimersECSSprites.atlas

A Celeste-style precision platformer: three single-screen rooms of forced dash-crystal chains over spike voids, wall jumps and coyote time. The Fsm drives a procedural sprite sheet, ECS owns the crystals, and Timers give forgiving jumps.

Guild Trader

UI.dialogDrag & dropLoot

Trade with Mara using draggable stack inventories, weighted loot and fair encounter bags.

Dungeon Scout

Flood fillLine of sightGrid

Explore generated rooms with reachable-region, grid-line and field-of-view recipes.

Lead Defender

Target leadingWavesParticles

An automated turret predicts moving targets through escalating radial enemy waves.

Checkpoint Rally

Physics2DGizmos.carAudio.engineSoundCamera

Drive a car around a long winding circuit, hitting numbered gates in order to complete laps. A dead-zone camera follows the car through a world bigger than the screen, with a corner minimap of the whole track and your best lap time.

Solitaire

ScenesUIDrag & dropFsm

Classic Klondike solitaire: drag-and-drop cards, undo, hints, win celebration and persisted stats — built with Scenes, Signals, Clock/Tween and more.
