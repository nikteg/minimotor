Poly Haven assets

Source: https://polyhaven.com/
License: CC0 1.0 Universal

The OBJ files in this directory were converted from Poly Haven's 1K glTF
downloads with Assimp. The diffuse maps were downloaded from the matching
Poly Haven model packages and retained alongside the converted OBJ files.

Poly Haven asks that products using its live API make the source clear to
users. These assets were downloaded and included locally, but the source is
credited here and in the render3d sample.
